A note about privacy

Your learning stays yours.

This website is a static guide. It does not ask for your name, email, school, or passwords. It does not upload files, use advertising trackers, or send your checklist to a server.

Your chosen path and completed steps are saved only in this browser’s local storage. Clearing browser data removes them. Links to courses open third-party websites with their own privacy practices.
